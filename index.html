Safety

Avoid Scams

🛑 Red Flags to Reject Immediately
  • Seller asks for payment via Zelle, Venmo, crypto, or Western Union — no buyer protection
  • Tickets sent as a PDF or photo before payment — PDFs can be copied infinitely
  • Price is suspiciously below market (e.g. $80 for an Argentina match) — too good to be true
  • Seller refuses to do a video call showing the FIFA app with the live QR code
  • Transfer attempted outside the official FIFA ticketing app — only official transfers are valid
  • Seller can’t provide the exact section, row, and seat number before you pay
