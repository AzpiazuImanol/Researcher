How It Works

Getting Your Tickets

Official tickets are sold exclusively through FIFA's ticketing platform. Here's the process.

1
Create a FIFA Account
Register on the official FIFA ticketing portal with a valid ID and payment method.
2
Apply in a Ballot
High-demand matches use a ballot system. Apply during the window and wait for the draw result.
3
First-Come, First-Served
Remaining tickets go on general sale. Be ready at the exact time — they sell out fast.
4
Receive E-Tickets
Your tickets are delivered digitally to your FIFA account. Add to Apple/Google Wallet.
5
Attend & Enjoy
Present your QR code at the turnstiles. Tickets are non-transferable and ID-linked.
