Safety

Avoid Scams

🛑 Red Flags — Walk Away Immediately
  • Payment requested via Zelle, Venmo, crypto, or Western Union — zero buyer protection
  • Ticket sent as PDF or screenshot before payment — PDFs can be duplicated endlessly
  • Price is suspiciously low (e.g. $80 Cat.2 for Argentina) — it’s a scam
  • Seller refuses video call showing live FIFA app QR code ticking in real time
  • Transfer outside the official FIFA app — only app-to-app transfers are valid at turnstiles
  • Seller can’t confirm Section, Row, and Seat number before payment
  • Pressure tactics: “I have 3 other buyers, decide in 10 minutes”
